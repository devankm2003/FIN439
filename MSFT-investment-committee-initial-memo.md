# Microsoft Corporation (NASDAQ: MSFT)
## Preliminary Investment Committee Memo — Watch / Defer

**As of:** August 31, 2025  
**Decision requested:** Authorize full valuation diligence; do not initiate a
position until the committee receives a filing-backed enterprise-to-equity
valuation range and downside case.

## Recommendation

**Watch / defer.** Microsoft’s fiscal 2025 filing supports a high-quality
business case for further work: Microsoft Cloud revenue reached $168.9 billion
(+23% year over year), and Azure and other cloud-services revenue grew 34%.
However, management also identifies continued AI-infrastructure investment and
growing AI product usage as a source of gross-margin pressure. Without a
complete forecast of reinvestment, margins, and free cash flow—and a
market-price comparison—an initiation recommendation would be premature.

## Decision context

This memo addresses whether an investment committee with no existing Microsoft
position should advance MSFT to full fundamental valuation. The valuation
object will be enterprise value, reconciled to common equity value and then to
value per diluted common share.

## Enterprise-to-equity bridge discipline

The final valuation will apply the following architecture:

`Equity value = Enterprise value + non-operating assets − debt − debt-like claims − non-common claims`

`Value per diluted share = Equity value ÷ diluted common shares`

For Microsoft, the bridge is not yet complete. The diligence work must resolve
the treatment of short-term investments, operating cash, debt carrying value
versus contractual principal, leases, other claims, and the OpenAI investment.

### Synthetic enterprise-to-equity bridge check

This known class example is a calculation check, not a Microsoft valuation
(all amounts in $M):

`Equity value = 1,000 enterprise value + 100 non-operating cash - 250 debt - 20 minority interest - 10 unfunded pension = 820`

`Value per diluted share = 820 / 100 diluted shares = $8.20`

The bridge reconciles: $1,000 of enterprise value becomes $820 of common equity
value after the $100 non-operating asset and the $280 of senior or non-common
claims are included.

## Investment case to test

The core question is whether cloud and AI demand can sustain elevated revenue
growth while Microsoft earns an attractive return on the capital required for
datacenters and related infrastructure. The positive case depends on durable
cloud consumption, operating leverage, and free-cash-flow conversion. The
downside case depends on a weaker monetization path, lower cloud margins, or
persistently higher reinvestment than the market currently assumes.

## Principal risks and falsification conditions

1. **AI-capital-intensity risk:** incremental cloud revenue may require more
   infrastructure capital than assumed, depressing free cash flow and returns
   on invested capital.
2. **Margin risk:** continued AI infrastructure investment and product-usage
   costs may prevent gross-margin recovery.
3. **Valuation risk:** strong reported growth can still produce an unattractive
   investment if the market price already embeds a more favorable outcome.
4. **Bridge risk:** treating all cash and short-term investments as excess, or
   omitting debt-like claims, could overstate equity value.

The watch/defer position should be rejected if a conservative, normalized
valuation shows inadequate downside protection after these risks. It should
advance to an initiation decision only if the filing-backed valuation range
exceeds the contemporaneous market price by a committee-defined margin of
safety.

## Required work before an initiation decision

1. Build a three-statement, filing-backed base year and normalize material
   non-recurring or basis-change items.
2. Forecast revenue, operating margin, D&A, capital expenditures, and working
   capital under base, downside, and slower-monetization cases.
3. Calculate FCFF and WACC using dated, documented inputs; produce terminal
   growth and WACC sensitivities.
4. Complete the enterprise-to-equity bridge, documenting every included and
   excluded claim.
5. Compare the resulting per-share valuation range with the August 31, 2025
   market price and return with a clear initiate, continue-to-watch, or reject
   recommendation.

## Committee ask

Approve limited diligence to complete the valuation and downside cases. No
capital allocation is requested at this stage.

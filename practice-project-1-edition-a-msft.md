## Decision and user

Advise a buy-side investment committee with no current Microsoft position
whether to begin fundamental valuation work that could lead to an initial
position in Microsoft Corporation (`MSFT`).

## Company and valuation date

- **Entity:** Microsoft Corporation
- **Ticker:** MSFT
- **Valuation date:** August 31, 2025
- **Valuation object:** Enterprise value, bridged to common equity value and
  value per diluted common share.

## Initial thesis — Watch / defer

Microsoft merits continued valuation work, but I would **watch / defer** an
initiation decision until a valuation range tests whether cloud and AI-related
growth can earn adequate returns on the company’s materially higher
infrastructure investment. The filing shows strong cloud-led growth, while
also identifying continued AI-infrastructure investment as a gross-margin
pressure.

## Synthetic enterprise-to-equity bridge check — known class answer

The class synthetic case reconciles as follows (all values in $M):

`Equity value = 1,000 EV + 100 non-operating cash − 250 debt − 20 minority interest − 10 unfunded pension = 820`

`Per-share value = 820 / 100 diluted shares = $8.20`

This is a calculation check only; it is not a Microsoft valuation. For MSFT,
the final bridge will require an explicit policy for short-term investments,
debt carrying value versus principal, lease liabilities, and any other
non-common claims.

## Consequential assumptions

1. Microsoft Cloud and Azure growth can be forecast from the FY2025 base only
   after separating recurring demand from AI-capacity buildout effects.
2. A meaningful portion of cash and short-term investments will be available
   as non-operating value, subject to a later operating-cash and restriction
   analysis.
3. The diluted weighted-average share count is an appropriate starting
   convention for a fiscal-year earnings/FCFF valuation, pending reconciliation
   to a current diluted-share estimate.
4. Debt and debt-like claims will be classified consistently with how lease
   costs and financing items are treated in FCFF.

## Unknowns and reversal conditions

- Whether AI infrastructure spending produces durable returns above its cost of
  capital or permanently lowers cloud margins.
- How much of reported investments is restricted or required for operations.
- The appropriate treatment of leases, other claims, and the OpenAI investment
  in the enterprise-to-equity bridge.
- The market price, cost of capital, forecast horizon, terminal growth, and
  resulting valuation range as of August 31, 2025.

The watch/defer view would turn negative if a normalized forecast shows that
cloud growth cannot offset investment-driven margin compression and required
reinvestment; it could turn into an initiation recommendation only if a
conservative valuation range exceeds the market price with an adequate margin
of safety.

## Research plan

1. Build a filing-backed evidence ledger for revenue, EBIT, tax rate, D&A,
   capital expenditures, and operating working capital; identify and label
   non-recurring or basis-change items.
2. Forecast cloud revenue, margin, and capital intensity under base, downside,
   and slower-AI-monetization cases.
3. Estimate FCFF and WACC with source dates and a transparent terminal-value
   sensitivity table.
4. Reconcile the enterprise-to-equity bridge line by line, including cash,
   investments, debt, leases, and non-common claims, and select a consistent
   diluted-share convention.
5. Compare the resulting per-share range with the August 31, 2025 market price
   and define initiation, defer, and rejection triggers.
